## Summary (max 250)
Tally turns promises buried in Jira issues and Confluence pages into tracked commitments. A person confirms each one, links the Jira work that delivers it, and Tally flags risk with nine plain rules and drafts customer updates for approval.

## More details (max 1000)
Sales and customer success teams promise things to customers every day: an SSO date, a migration plan, an integration before renewal. Those promises end up in a page or a comment, and nobody owns them until the customer asks.

Tally scans the Jira projects and Confluence spaces you choose and lists possible promises with the exact quote. A person confirms, edits or rejects each one; nothing is tracked automatically. Confirmed commitments get an owner, a due date and linked Jira work, and their status follows that work.

Nine rule-based risk flags (overdue, due soon, no owner, blocked work and more) each give a plain reason. The Teamwork Graph shows related work. Customer updates are drafted from stored facts only, and a person approves and sends them.

Tally Agent in Rovo answers "what is at risk and why", and Tally's tools are available to other AI assistants through Atlassian MCP. Runs on Atlassian: no data leaves your site.
